# KYC Document Requirements for Verification

**Document ID:** KB-DOC-004
**Topic:** `kyc_document_requirements`

Mandatory Customer Due Diligence requires a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) along with Aadhaar-based digital biometric authentication. For address verification where current residential address differs from Aadhaar, acceptable Officially Valid Documents (OVD) include a Passport, Voter ID, or utility bill within three months. Self-employed business applicants must additionally provide Goods and Services Tax (GST) registration certificates and the previous two years' Income Tax Returns (ITR).
