# Non-Resident Indian (NRI) Account Eligibility

**Document ID:** KB-DOC-012
**Topic:** `nri_account_eligibility`

Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) are eligible to open Non-Resident External (NRE) and Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) accounts. NRE accounts permit full repatriability of both principal and interest earnings with total Indian income tax exemption on accrued interest. NRO accounts are mandatory for managing rupee income earned inside India (such as rent or pensions) and are subject to Indian withholding tax and FEMA remittance ceilings.
