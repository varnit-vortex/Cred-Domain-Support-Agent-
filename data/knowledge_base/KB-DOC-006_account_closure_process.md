# Account and Line-of-Credit Closure Process

**Document ID:** KB-DOC-006
**Topic:** `account_closure_process`

Account closure or cancellation of active credit lines requires total liquidation of all outstanding principal, interest dues, and unbilled charges. A formal closure request must be initiated via verified email or the in-app support portal, followed by authentication via two-factor OTP. Once settled, a digitally signed No Objection Certificate (NOC) and full No Dues Certificate (NDC) are issued to the member within seven business days.
