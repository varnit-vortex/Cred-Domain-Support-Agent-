# Joint Account Mandates and Operational Rules

**Document ID:** KB-DOC-011
**Topic:** `joint_account_rules`

Joint accounts can be created with spouse, parents, or adult siblings under 'Either or Survivor', 'Former or Survivor', or 'Jointly Operated' operational mandates. All primary co-borrowers share equal joint-and-several financial liability for any credit facilities or overdrafts linked to the account. Mandate modifications, account conversions, or credit closure requests require unanimous written authorization and biometric/OTP approval from all registered account holders.
